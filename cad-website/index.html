03 / How we work

From first conversation to working software

Each stage gives you something concrete to review, from the initial project outline to the release and handover.

  1. 01

    Understand your goals

    We discuss your workflow, existing tools, and the problem you want to solve. This initial conversation helps us understand your goals, priorities, and constraints before preparing a proposal.

    We establish: project goals, priorities, and next steps.
  2. 02

    Agree the proposal

    We include detailed discovery in the project proposal, alongside the proposed approach, deliverables, estimate, and timeline. Once the proposal is agreed, discovery establishes the requirements and scope before development begins.

    You receive: a proposal covering discovery, deliverables, estimate, and timeline.
  3. 03

    Build and review

    We share working demonstrations as development progresses. You can try the software, give feedback, and review progress against the agreed scope.

    You receive: working demonstrations to review.
  4. 04

    Release and hand over

    We test the agreed functionality, release the software, and provide documentation and handover. The proposal defines the post-launch issue-resolution period and scope. Ongoing maintenance is agreed separately.

    You receive: a tested release, documentation, and handover.
