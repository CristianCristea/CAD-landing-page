03 / How we work

From agreed scope to working software

Each stage gives you something concrete to review, from the initial requirements to the release and handover.

  1. 01

    Define the scope

    We review your workflow, existing tools, and the problem you want to solve. Together, we document the requirements, set priorities, and agree what the project will cover.

    You receive: documented requirements and agreed scope.
  2. 02

    Agree the proposal

    We set out the proposed solution, deliverables, estimate, and timeline so you can review the approach and agree the plan before development begins.

    You receive: a proposal with deliverables, estimate, and timeline.
  3. 03

    Build and review

    We share working demonstrations as development progresses. You can try the software, give feedback, and review progress against the agreed scope.

    You receive: working demonstrations to review.
  4. 04

    Release and hand over

    We test the agreed functionality, release the software, and provide documentation and handover. The proposal defines the post-launch issue-resolution period and scope. Ongoing maintenance is agreed separately.

    You receive: a tested release, documentation, and handover.
